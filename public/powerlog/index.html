Frequently asked questions

Is my data private?

Yes. PowerLog stores everything locally on your device using SQLite. There is no account, no server, and no sync — your training and health data never leaves your phone.

What happens if I switch phones?

Export your full history to CSV from Settings, then import it on your new device. Nothing is tied to a cloud account, so you're always in control of your backups.

What platforms are supported?

PowerLog runs natively on both Android and iOS.
