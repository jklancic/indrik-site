Data collection

PowerLog does not have a server, an account system, or analytics. Everything you log — workouts, body measurements, nutrition, and sleep — is stored locally on your device in a SQLite database and is never transmitted anywhere.

Crash reporting

PowerLog uses Firebase Crashlytics to report crashes and non-fatal errors so issues can be fixed. Crash reports may include device model, OS version, and app version, but not your personal training data.

Data export

You can export all of your data to CSV at any time from Settings and share or store it however you choose. PowerLog has no way to access files once exported.

Data deletion

Uninstalling the app permanently deletes all locally stored data. There is no account to delete because none exists.
