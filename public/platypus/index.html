Platypus

Platypus is a separate venture co-founded by Jernej Klančič, who serves as its CTO.

Platypus is an integrated product development and lifecycle management tool for healthcare and life sciences teams across the pharmaceutical, nutraceutical, food, and cosmetic industries.

Who it's for

  • Pharmaceutical
  • Nutraceutical
  • Food
  • Cosmetics

What it covers

  • Raw material science
  • Early-stage formulation development
  • Regulatory and quality compliance
  • Cost and pricing
  • Manufacturing process development
  • Documentation management
  • Supply chain
  • Commercial manufacturing
